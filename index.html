Selected Projects

01 MT7630E-MODERN Linux driver
the story

The MT7630E wifi chip lost mainline kernel support years ago, leaving a pile of old laptops with dead wifi. I picked up the abandoned out-of-tree driver, ported it against current kernel APIs, and fixed the DMA and interrupt handling that had rotted out.

the result

It builds cleanly on modern kernels and a handful of people with old ThinkPads have wifi again. Small fix, but it's the kind of unglamorous maintenance that keeps hardware usable past its "official" lifespan.

02 Disflux Audio DSP
the story

I wanted a distortion plugin that didn't just clip a waveform, but let you shape the harmonic character directly. That turned into a few months of reading DSP papers and re-deriving waveshaping math I only half-understood on the first pass.

technical details

Runs multiple waveshaping stages with 4x oversampling to keep aliasing under control, a tone stage built from cascaded biquad filters, and a wet/dry path designed to stay phase-coherent. Written in C++ against the JUCE framework.

03 Plasma Audio plugin
the story

My first real audio plugin, built before I understood most of the constraints that matter for real-time audio. It taught me why allocations on the audio thread are a bad idea, the hard way.

honest reflection

It works, and people have used it, but the UI code is a mess and the CPU usage is higher than it should be. I keep it around unpolished on purpose — it's a useful reminder of where I started.
